# Contributing

Thank you for improving PriceSignal.

1. Open an issue describing the pricing decision, evidence route, and proposed behavior.
2. Keep the interface decision-first and the evidence tier visible.
3. Add a deterministic analytical fixture for every statistical change.
4. Use synthetic or openly licensed data only.
5. Run `python -m pytest`, `python -m ruff check .`, and `python -m build` before proposing a change.

Do not add claims that robust covariance, a flexible curve, a large sample, or an optimization routine creates causal identification. Do not reproduce course slides, proprietary templates, licensed datasets, or confidential pricing evidence.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
